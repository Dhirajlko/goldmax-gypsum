BEST PRACTICES

Standard Application Workflow

Achieving mirror-smooth perfection in 5 simple, time-saving steps.

01

Surface Cleaning

Clean the masonry or RCC surface of dust, oil, and loose particles. Pre-wet the surface with light water mist to control suction.

02

Bond Coat on RCC

For smooth RCC ceilings or columns, brush on Goldmax Bond Coat to create a mechanical key. Allow 24 hours to cure.

03

Water Mixing

In a clean tub, add water first (~17-18 Litres per 25Kg bag). Sprinkle Goldmax Gypsum Plaster uniformly. Mix to a smooth paste.

04

Single Coat Leveling

Apply with a stainless steel trowel with firm pressure from bottom to top. Level with aluminium straight edge (bull marks / strips).

05

Mirror Finishing

When the plaster begins to stiffen, finish with a steel trowel with light water sprinkling. No POP or putty required. Ready to paint!
